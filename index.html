Market Transformation

The 1970s Turbine Dilemma vs. Electric Hydrofoil Flight

While civil aviation continuously upgrades to ultra-efficient fleets, high-speed maritime routes in Hong Kong still rely on aging 1970s gas-turbine drivetrains burning ~450+ gallons of fuel per hour.

With rising marine gas oil (MGO) costs and fixed bridge-competition fares, traditional fleet models face severe structural losses. Baywing replaces thermal burn with hydrofoil aerodynamic lift and battery-electric efficiency.

Execution Strategy

4-Stage Engineering Tech Tree

A disciplined hardware development tree designed to eliminate technical risk before scaling to high-capacity passenger routes.

Stage 0

Virtual Flight Control

CFD hydrodynamic modeling, digital twin development, and dynamic simulation for Lingdingyang sea swells.

Stage 1 (Current)

The Mule Testbed

10–12m prototype demonstrator. Validates real-world active foil trim, ultrasonic wave-height sensors, and thermal management.

Stage 2

Domestic Pilot

Commercial pilot deployment on South Lantau / Outlying Island routes utilizing intermediate 1.5 MW charging infrastructure.

Stage 3

Flagship Baywing X-1

Full-scale 35+ knot 150-pax hydrofoil launch on the flagship HK–Macau cross-boundary corridor with 3.8 MW MCS.
