2. Market Disruption & Economic Moat

Why Legacy Diesel Ferry Operators face Extinction

Legacy fast ferries (e.g. TurboJET, Cotai Water Jet) rely on multi-megawatt diesel gas turbines and waterjets pushing heavy displacement hulls through water. High marine fuel costs, severe wake restrictions in harbor channels, and escalating diesel turbine overhaul expenses have rendered traditional models unprofitable.

80%

Hydrodynamic Drag Reduction

At speeds above 18 knots, the EF-150 lifts completely out of the water. Drag drops by 80% compared to high-speed catamaran hulls, allowing 38-knot flight using a fraction of the power.

Catamaran Hull Power: 4,800 kW
EF-150 Hydrofoil Power: 1,050 kW
78%

Direct Operational Energy Savings

Electricity costs per passenger-mile are up to 78% lower than marine gas oil (MGO). Electric direct-drive rim pods also eliminate gearbox oil, drive shafts, and complex marine turbine overhauls.

Legacy MGO Diesel: ~$22.50 / NM
EF-150 Electric Power: ~$4.90 / NM
0.12m

Unrestricted Harbor Clearance

Conventional ferries produce heavy wake wash, forcing speed limits (10-12 kts) in Victoria Harbour and coastal estuaries. EF-150’s submerged foils generate near-zero wake (<15cm), permitting full speed cruising right to the pier.

Victoria Harbour Legacy Speed: 10 Knots Limit
EF-150 Permitted Speed: 38 Knots Direct

4. Integrated Powertrain & Infrastructure

Propulsion & Megawatt Charging (MCS) Architecture

The EF-150 combines dual direct-drive submerged rim-drive propulsors with an 800V solid-state marine energy system, backed by automated shore-side Megawatt Charging (MCS).

Submerged Rim-Drive Propulsion 1.5 MW TOTAL RATING

Mounted directly inside the submerged hydrofoil nacelles. Gearless permanent-magnet rim motors eliminate internal drive shafts, thrust bearings, and mechanical drag while providing instant torque vectoring for tight harbor docking maneuvers.

  • Motor Type: Dual Permanent Magnet Rim Drives
  • Combined Continuous Power: 1,500 kW (2,010 HP)
  • Acoustic Cavitation Index: < 42 dB (Ultra-Silent Marine)
  • Maintenance Interval: 20,000 Operating Hours
Megawatt Shore Charging System (MCS) 2.4 MW CHARGE RATE

Designed for turnkey installation at existing ferry terminals (e.g. Hong Kong Macau Ferry Terminal, SkyPier, Shekou Ferry Port). Features automated robotic connection arms that engage within 20 seconds of berth securing.

  • Charging Standard: MCS (CharIN Marine Standard)
  • Peak Power Output: 2,400 kW DC Direct
  • Turnaround Time (20% to 90%): 38 Minutes
  • Grid Storage Buffer Option: 2.0 MWh Containerized BESS
⚡

Redundant Dual-Bus Architecture

Isolated dual 800V DC buses ensure complete propulsion & flight control availability even during single-string battery cell isolation.
