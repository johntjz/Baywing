eFoiler Drivetrain & Megawatt Charging

Combining aerospace hydrodynamics with 800V marinised solid-state battery architecture and shore-to-ship megawatt infrastructure.

Submerged Direct-Drive Pods

Submerged electric pod units elevate the vessel hull above water, cutting friction drag by 80%. Produces less than 15cm wake height at 35 knots, completely eliminating shore erosion and protecting marine habitats.

  • Drag Reduction: 80% Less Friction
  • Wake Height: < 15 cm @ 35 kts
  • Vibration: 6x Smoother Ride

800V Marinised Battery & BMS

High energy density marinised solid-state & LiFePO4 packs with liquid isolation cooling, multi-stage off-gas early warning detection, and compliance with IEC 62619/62620 & UN38.3 standards.

  • System Voltage: 800V Architecture
  • Safety Standard: IEC 62619 / UN38.3
  • Protection: Off-gas Detection

Megawatt Charging System (MCS)

Dockside Megawatt Charging System (MCS) with battery-buffered Booster Box. Delivers up to 1.2MW ultra-fast charging to fully top-up batteries in 45 minutes without overloading local pier power grids.

  • Turnaround: 45 Mins Full Charge
