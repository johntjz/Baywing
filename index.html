▸ Software & Flight Control Architecture (FCS)

The Baywing EF-150 utilizes a proprietary closed-loop, fly-by-wire Flight Control System running at 100 Hz. Designed in accordance with DNV High Speed Light Craft (HSLC) software safety rules, it operates on a triple-redundant logic structure.

Sensor & Perception Stack Dual forward-looking LiDAR wave profilers map incoming wave heights at 200 Hz. Triple-redundant aviation-grade IMUs provide real-time 6-DOF (Degrees of Freedom) data to preemptively adjust foil angles before wave contact.
Active Ride Control (ARC) PID + Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithms dynamically actuate T-foil flaps 100 times per second, ensuring zero motion sickness and maintaining a strict <0.15m wake wash profile for harbor exemptions.

⚡ Floating BESS Buffer Architecture

Solves the peak-power bottleneck at public piers without requiring multi-million-dollar shore grid upgrades. A self-contained, liquid-cooled battery energy storage pontoon buffers the high-power demands of rapid vessel turnaround.

Grid & Power Balance Draws a highly stable, continuous 300 kW load from the local HK Electric grid over a 2-hour loop. This energy is buffered into a 1.6 MWh LFP battery bank to seamlessly deliver a massive 1.5 MW DC peak charge to the vessel.
MCS Automated Interface Features an automated Megawatt Charging System (MCS) pantograph that connects to the vessel upon docking, achieving a 10% to 90% SOC charge in under 45 minutes to support 8 daily round trips.
