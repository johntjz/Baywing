Engineering Precision

The Aero-Marine System

Combining aerospace aerodynamics with deep-water hydrodynamics for low-drag, high-efficiency maritime operation.

Submerged Electric Foiling Pods

Submerged direct-drive electric motor pods lift the ship hull completely clear of the surface at cruising speeds. This slashes hydrodynamic friction drag by up to 80%, reducing energy demands while generating zero wake turbulence.

Solid-State Marine Battery System

High energy density solid-state marine-grade lithium-ion battery packs (up to 2.5 MWh capacity) configured with redundant thermal liquid isolation, designed for high charge acceptance and thousands of marine operational cycles.

Shore-to-Ship Megawatt Charging (MCS)

Designed specifically for integration with Hong Kong public piers and GBA ferry terminals. Floating pontoon rapid-charging units replenish batteries during standard 45-minute passenger turnaround windows without grid strain.
