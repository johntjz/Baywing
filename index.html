A data-driven proposal to solve the OPEX crisis of legacy marine transport in the Greater Bay Area. Utilizing computer-controlled carbon-fiber hydrofoils, the EF-150 architecture eliminates hydrodynamic drag and heavily reduces operational energy consumption to create viable, high-margin commuter routes.

Defensible OPEX Advantages

Legacy fast ferries rely on multi-megawatt diesel gas turbines pushing heavy displacement hulls. High marine fuel costs and escalating diesel turbine overhaul expenses heavily compress operating margins.
